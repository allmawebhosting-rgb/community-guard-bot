ALLMA SAFETY AI
MASTER BUILD — PHASE 8
SAFETY INTELLIGENCE, ANALYTICS & PREVENTION ENGINE
============================================================

IMPORTANT:

This is PHASE 8.

DO NOT rebuild, remove, or break any functionality from
PHASES 1–7.

Extend the existing Allma Safety AI system.

The goal of this phase is to create an advanced
SAFETY INTELLIGENCE AND PREVENTION PLATFORM.

Allma should not only respond to emergencies.

It should help authorized authorities:

• Understand incident patterns
• Identify emerging trends
• Detect possible clusters
• Allocate resources
• Identify areas requiring attention
• Monitor response performance
• Generate intelligence reports
• Support prevention planning
• Coordinate different levels of command

IMPORTANT:

AI provides intelligence and recommendations.

AI MUST NOT make final law-enforcement decisions.

Humans and authorized authorities remain responsible
for operational decisions.
