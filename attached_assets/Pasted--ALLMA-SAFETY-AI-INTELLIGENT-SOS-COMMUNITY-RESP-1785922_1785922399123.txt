ALLMA SAFETY AI – INTELLIGENT SOS COMMUNITY RESPONSE

==================================================

Enhance the SOS feature to intelligently coordinate nearby help while respecting privacy and emergency response best practices.

==================================================

SOS ACTIVATION

The moment the user taps the SOS button:

• Activate Emergency Mode immediately.

• Display an emergency animation.

• Request precise GPS location if permission has not already been granted.

• Begin live location updates during the emergency session.

AI says:

"Emergency mode activated.

I'm locating you and determining the fastest available assistance."

==================================================

AI ANALYSIS

The AI automatically determines:

• Emergency type

• Current location

• District

• Village

• Police jurisdiction

• Nearby police station

• Nearby hospital

• Nearby fire station

• Estimated response times

==================================================

SMART COMMUNITY RESPONSE

After determining the emergency type, the AI searches for nearby Allma Safety AI users who have opted in to receive emergency notifications.

Search radius is configurable based on the emergency.

Medical

500m

Road Accident

1km

Fire

2km

Missing Person

5km

Only notify users who have enabled Community Emergency Alerts.

==================================================

AI DECISION ENGINE

Before notifying anyone, determine:

• Is this safe for community assistance?

• Does this require only official responders?

• Which nearby users are eligible?

• Which verified responders are nearby?

• Who is closest?

• Who is available?

Never notify users if doing so could increase danger.

IF A NEARBY USER ACCEPTS

The AI asks:

"Thank you for offering to help.

Can you safely assist without putting yourself in danger?"

If YES

Display navigation.

Share only the information necessary to assist.

Keep both parties updated.

LIVE CHAT

The AI continues talking to the person throughout the emergency.

Examples:

"I've notified nearby assistance."

"Official emergency services have been contacted."

"A nearby verified responder has accepted."

"Stay where you are if it is safe."

"I'm updating your live location."

The AI should continue asking one question at a time while providing reassurance and guidance.

==================================================

PRIVACY

Protect all users.

Never reveal:

Exact GPS

Phone numbers

Names

Personal details

unless sharing is necessary for emergency coordination and consistent with the user's choices and applicable policies.

==================================================

ENDING THE EMERGENCY

Once the emergency is resolved:

Stop live location sharing.

Stop nearby notifications.

Generate an incident summary.

Save the emergency report.

Ask the user whether they need any additional assistance.

GOAL

The SOS feature should become an intelligent emergency coordination system that:

• Locates the user.

• Understands the emergency.

• Guides the user one question at a time.

• Coordinates with official responders.

• Optionally alerts nearby opt-in community responders when appropriate.

• Keeps the user informed until the emergency is resolved.
