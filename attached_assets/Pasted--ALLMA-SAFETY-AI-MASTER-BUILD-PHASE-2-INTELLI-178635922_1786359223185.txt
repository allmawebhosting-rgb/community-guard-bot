DO NOT REBUILD OR DELETE PHASE 1.

The goal of this phase is to create an extremely intelligent, fast, calm and premium SOS experience.

When a user activates SOS, Allma should behave like an intelligent emergency assistant.

It should:

• Immediately activate emergency mode
• Acquire the user's real location with permission
• Understand what is happening
• Ask one question at a time
• Determine emergency severity
• Determine emergency category
• Decide what assistance is appropriate
• Prepare the emergency information
• Keep the citizen informed
• Prepare the system for the emergency-contact and responder calling system in PHASE 3

1. SOS ENTRY POINT
==================================================

Create a highly visible SOS button throughout the citizen application.

The button must be:

• Extremely clear
• Easy to reach
• Accessible
• Visually premium
• Fast
• Responsive

Before activation, show a subtle confirmation mechanism only if required by the existing UX.

Do NOT introduce unnecessary confirmation screens that could delay a genuine emergency.

==================================================
7. LOCATION SECURITY
==================================================

Never expose the user's exact GPS coordinates to ordinary users.

Exact location should only be available to:

• The citizen
• Authorized emergency personnel
• Authorized responders when appropriate
• Systems explicitly authorized by the emergency workflow

Nearby community responders should initially receive approximate location.

More precise location may be shared after an authorized responder accepts the emergency, according to the user's settings and safety rules.

10. ONE QUESTION AT A TIME
==================================================

THIS IS CRITICAL.

The AI must NEVER overwhelm the user with a long questionnaire.

Ask one question.

Wait for response.

Analyze.

Then ask the next most important question.

Example:

Question 1:

"What is happening?"

User:

"Someone broke into the house."

Question 2:

"Are you currently inside the house?"

User:

"Yes."

Question 3:

"Are you injured?"

User:

"No."

Question 4:

"Can you safely move to a secure location?"

User:

"Yes."

The AI then changes the response strategy.

15. SAFETY GUIDANCE
==================================================

The AI should provide calm, context-appropriate instructions.

Examples:

"If it is safe, move away from the danger."

"Do not confront the person."

"Stay somewhere visible and secure if possible."

"Do not put yourself at greater risk to collect information."

For violent incidents:

NEVER instruct ordinary citizens to confront suspects.

==================================================
16. MEDICAL TRIAGE
==================================================

For medical emergencies ask relevant questions such as:

What happened?

Is the person conscious?

Are they breathing normally?

Are they bleeding heavily?

Is the person trapped?

Are you with them?

Only ask questions relevant to the situation.

Do not produce unnecessary medical interrogation.

Provide conservative first-aid guidance where appropriate.

Always encourage professional emergency assistance for serious situations.

==================================================
17. FIRE TRIAGE
==================================================

Ask:

Is there a fire?

Are people trapped?

Are you inside the building?

Is smoke present?

Can you safely leave?

Prioritize evacuation and official fire services.

Do NOT instruct users to enter burning buildings.

==================================================
18. CRIME / ATTACK TRIAGE
==================================================

Ask:

What is happening?

Are you currently in danger?

Is the suspect still nearby?

Are you injured?

Can you move somewhere safe?

Do not encourage confrontation.

Do not instruct citizens to chase suspects.

19. ROAD ACCIDENT
==================================================

Are people injured?

Is anyone unconscious?

Is anyone trapped?
