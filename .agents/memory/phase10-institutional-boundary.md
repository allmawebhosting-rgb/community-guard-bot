Phase 10 command surfaces must aggregate authorized operational records without presenting Allma as police, government or an emergency service. National, regional, district and station scopes are permission boundaries, not permission grants. Institutional records, integrations, resources and system statuses must be shown as unconfigured or unavailable when they do not exist.

**Why:** The Phase 10 specification prepares the product for institutional deployment and handover while keeping official decisions, dispatch, enforcement and emergency operations with authorized institutions.

**How to apply:** Use existing incident, dispatch and officer data for live KPIs; keep exact locations private; require human approval for official actions; separate demo/staging/production; and never fill missing organizations, responders, alerts, integrations or health statuses with fabricated values.
