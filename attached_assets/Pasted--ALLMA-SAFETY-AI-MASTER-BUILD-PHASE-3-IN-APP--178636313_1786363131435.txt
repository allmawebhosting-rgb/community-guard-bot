PHASE 1 created:

PHASE 2 created:

DO NOT rebuild or delete these systems.

Extend them.

The purpose of this phase is to build the REAL-TIME IN-APP EMERGENCY COMMUNICATION SYSTEM.

When a citizen activates SOS, Allma should intelligently determine who can help and attempt to contact appropriate people through the Allma app.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT implement fake calls as real calls.

The architecture must support genuine in-app voice communication using a proper WebRTC/voice infrastructure.

If production voice infrastructure is not configured yet, create a clearly labelled DEMO CALL MODE and keep the integration boundary ready for a real provider.

Never claim that a real call occurred when it did not.

==================================================
1. CORE CONCEPT
==================================================

Allma has two emergency networks.

NETWORK 1:

Trusted Emergency Circle

People personally selected and accepted by the citizen.

NETWORK 2:

Opt-in Community Responders

Verified or approved Allma users who have explicitly agreed to receive emergency assistance requests.

The AI may use either network depending on:

Emergency type
Severity
Distance
Availability
Skills
Permissions
Responder status
Safety

==================================================
2. CALLING PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Do NOT randomly call nearby Allma users.

Only contact users who:

• Have explicitly opted in
• Have accepted the required emergency permissions
• Are eligible for the emergency category
• Are available
• Are not blocked
• Are not already handling another emergency
• Can legally and safely participate
