NATIONAL EMERGENCY NETWORK & GOVERNMENT INTEGRATION
============================================================

IMPORTANT:

This is PHASE 10.

DO NOT rebuild, remove, or break functionality from
PHASES 1–9.

Extend the existing Allma Safety AI architecture.

The objective of this phase is to prepare Allma Safety AI
for institutional deployment and eventual handover to
authorized government and emergency-response authorities.

The system must be designed as infrastructure that can
be operated by authorized institutions.

ALLMA AI IS THE TECHNOLOGY AND INTELLIGENCE LAYER.

IT IS NOT THE POLICE.

IT IS NOT THE GOVERNMENT.

IT IS NOT AN EMERGENCY SERVICE.

Official institutions remain responsible for decisions,
dispatch, enforcement, investigations, and emergency
operations.
