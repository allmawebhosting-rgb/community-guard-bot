ALLMA SAFETY AI
MASTER BUILD — PHASE 1
ONBOARDING + TRUSTED EMERGENCY NETWORK

IMPORTANT:
Do not redesign unrelated existing features.
Do not replace existing functionality that already works.
Build this as a production-quality foundation for the Allma Safety AI emergency system.

The application is an AI-powered public safety platform.

The most important concept is:

EVERY USER BUILDS A TRUSTED EMERGENCY CIRCLE.

When that user later activates SOS, Allma can intelligently contact eligible members of that circle through secure in-app communication, based on the emergency type, availability, proximity, permissions and safety rules.

This is NOT a social network.

Connections exist primarily for emergency assistance.

16. EMERGENCY LOCATION PERMISSION
==================================================

Approximate location before acceptance.

==================================================

• See their own Emergency Circle
• See requests involving them
• Modify their own preferences
• Remove their own connections

Do not expose sensitive data through frontend queries.

Use server-side validation for sensitive actions.
