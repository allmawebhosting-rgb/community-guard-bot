# ALLMA SAFETY AI – PREMIUM UI / UX DESIGN SYSTEM

Redesign the entire application into a premium, modern AI chat experience. The interface should feel as polished as ChatGPT, Apple Intelligence, Linear, Arc Browser, and Notion AI while reflecting Uganda's identity in a tasteful way.

Do NOT use outdated cards, generic buttons, or cluttered layouts.

The entire UI should feel alive with subtle animations and premium transitions.
