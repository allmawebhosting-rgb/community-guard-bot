1. PURPOSE
==================================================

Transform Allma from an emergency chatbot into an intelligent CASE ASSISTANCE SYSTEM.

Allma should help authorized responders understand:

WHAT HAPPENED

WHERE IT HAPPENED

WHEN IT HAPPENED

WHO IS INVOLVED

WHAT THE CITIZEN REPORTED

WHAT HAS BEEN CONFIRMED

WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN

WHAT ACTIONS HAVE ALREADY BEEN TAKEN

WHAT QUESTIONS SHOULD BE ASKED NEXT

WHAT INFORMATION MAY BE RELEVANT

WHETHER OTHER CASES MAY BE CONNECTED

IMPORTANT:

Allma is an ASSISTANT.

It must never independently determine guilt, declare someone a criminal, fabricate evidence, or replace an authorized investigator/officer.

==================================================
