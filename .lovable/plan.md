# Allma Safety AI Narrated Video Presentation

Create a professional 15–18 minute video presentation from the approved 51-page pitch deck.

## Production

- Use every deck page in its existing order, preserving the verified real screenshots and Google Maps captures.
- Add restrained camera movement, page transitions, section markers, and emphasis treatments that match Allma’s black, white, yellow, and red visual identity.
- Write a concise narration script that follows the document’s claims exactly and clearly distinguishes live capability from planned work.
- Generate a warm East African English voiceover with clear, confident pitch pacing.
- Synchronize each page’s screen time and motion to its spoken section; keep screenshots readable rather than rushing through them.
- Add subtle background music only if it remains clearly beneath the narration.

## Quality checks

- Render and inspect representative frames throughout the full timeline.
- Verify voice clarity, narration/page synchronization, transitions, total duration, video dimensions, and final audio levels.
- Deliver the finished MP4 plus the narration script.

## Technical details

- Build the presentation as a reusable Remotion project under `remotion/`.
- Render at 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC audio.
- Export deliverables to `/mnt/documents/` without changing the application or source pitch PDF.
