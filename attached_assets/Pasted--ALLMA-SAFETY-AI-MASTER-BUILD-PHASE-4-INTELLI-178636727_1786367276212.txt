==================================================
1. CORE CONCEPT
==================================================

Allma must NOT simply search for the nearest user.

It should find:

THE RIGHT PERSON
AT THE RIGHT LOCATION
AT THE RIGHT TIME
WITH THE RIGHT CAPABILITY
WHO HAS EXPLICITLY OPTED IN.

A responder can be:

• First aider
• Community safety volunteer
• Fire safety responder
• Transport helper
• Search-and-rescue volunteer
• Child safety volunteer
• Local community responder
• Other approved emergency helper

Do NOT allow users to falsely represent themselves as police officers, doctors, paramedics or other official authorities.

Official qualifications must be verified separately.
