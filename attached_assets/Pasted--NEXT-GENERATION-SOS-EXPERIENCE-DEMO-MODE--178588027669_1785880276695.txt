==================================================

NEXT GENERATION SOS EXPERIENCE (DEMO MODE)

==================================================

For this demo, tapping the SOS button immediately starts an emergency session.

No long press is required.

As soon as the SOS button is tapped:

• Start emergency mode.

• Display beautiful transition animations.

• Show a full-screen emergency interface.

• Automatically request location permission.

• Begin tracking the user's live location once permission is granted.
