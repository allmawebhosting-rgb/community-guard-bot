ALLMA SAFETY AI – AI EMERGENCY ESCALATION ENGINE
==================================================

When a citizen taps SOS:

STEP 1

Immediately activate Emergency Mode.

Get live GPS.

Determine emergency type.

Begin AI conversation.

==================================================

STEP 2

The AI searches for nearby users who:

• Have enabled Emergency Response Mode
• Are online or recently active
• Have location sharing enabled
• Have accepted emergency call permissions

Rank responders by:

Distance

Availability

Verification

Response history

Medical skills (if applicable)

==================================================

STEP 3

The AI creates a responder priority list.

Example

1. Verified First Aider
250 metres

2. Community Volunteer
400 metres

3. Community Health Worker
600 metres

4. LC1 Chairperson

5. Police Station

6. Hospital

==================================================

STEP 4

The AI asks:

"I've found nearby emergency responders.

Would you like me to contact them?"

Buttons

Call Nearest Responder

Notify Nearby Responders

Contact Local Authority

Contact Police

Contact Ambulance

Cancel

==================================================

STEP 5

If confirmed

The app automatically places calls using the device's phone capabilities or VoIP if supported.

The AI continues coordinating until official responders arrive.

==================================================

SAFETY

For violent crimes

The AI should prioritize police and official emergency services.

Nearby volunteers should never be instructed to confront suspects.

Instead they may help by:

Guiding emergency services

Providing first aid after the scene is safe

Helping with evacuation

Waiting in a safe location

==================================================

FINAL GOAL

The AI intelligently coordinates the fastest available help while protecting both the person requesting help and the community responders.
