============================================================
ALLMA SAFETY AI
MASTER BUILD — PHASE 9
EMERGENCY COMMUNICATION & RESPONSE NETWORK
============================================================

IMPORTANT:

This is PHASE 9.

DO NOT rebuild or remove functionality from PHASES 1–8.

Extend the existing Allma Safety AI platform.

The goal of this phase is to create a reliable,
multi-level emergency communication system.

Allma should be able to coordinate an emergency
across multiple available response channels.

The system should intelligently determine:

1. WHO needs help
2. WHERE they are
3. WHAT appears to be happening
4. HOW serious the situation may be
5. WHO is available to help
6. WHO should be contacted first
7. WHEN to escalate
8. WHETHER someone acknowledged the emergency
9. WHAT happened afterward

IMPORTANT:

Allma AI coordinates communication.

It does NOT impersonate police, ambulance services,
fire services, or government authorities.

Do not claim an official integration unless an actual
integration exists and has been verified.
