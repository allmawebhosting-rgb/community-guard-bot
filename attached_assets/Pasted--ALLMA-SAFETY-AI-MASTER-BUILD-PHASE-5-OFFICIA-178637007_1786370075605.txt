1. PURPOSE
==================================================

Create the official authority coordination layer.

Allma must be able to prepare and route emergency cases to appropriate authorized authorities.

Potential authorities:

• Uganda Police Force
• Ambulance / medical emergency services
• Fire and rescue services
• Local government authorities
• LC1 / authorized local leadership
• Health facilities
• Other authorized emergency organizations

IMPORTANT:

Allma must NOT pretend to be an official authority.

Allma is a technology coordination platform.

Official integrations must only be activated after authorization.

==================================================
